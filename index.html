FAQ

Questions, Answered

Do I need a licence to rent out my Dubai property short-term?

Yes. Holiday homes in Dubai require a DTCM permit. Luxon Prime assists owners with the full application and compliance process.

What does property management include?

Listing creation, pricing, guest communication, check-in, housekeeping, maintenance, utilities coordination and detailed owner reporting.

Can I stay in my own property?

Absolutely. Block your own dates anytime through your owner dashboard or by contacting your account manager.

Do you offer monthly stays?

Yes — fully furnished, bills-inclusive stays from one month, ideal for professionals, relocations and extended visits.

Which areas do you cover?

All seven emirates, with a focus on Dubai (Downtown, Marina, Palm, Business Bay, Dubai Hills) and Abu Dhabi (Saadiyat, Yas, Al Reem).
